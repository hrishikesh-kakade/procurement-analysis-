# Supplier Onboarding and Evaluation

## Onboarding requirements
New suppliers must provide a registered business address, a tax registration number, a contact e-mail address and a bank account letter before they can be set to active status. Only active suppliers can receive purchase orders or submit quotations.

## Supplier scorecard
Each supplier receives a score from 0 to 100. Fulfilment rate (units received compared with units ordered) and on-time delivery rate each count for 40 percent. The RFQ win rate counts for 20 percent. Ratings are A for scores of 85 or more, B for 70 or more, C for 50 or more, and D below 50.

## Blacklisting and inactive suppliers
Suppliers with repeated quality failures or late deliveries can be set to blacklisted by a procurement manager. A supplier that has purchase orders cannot be deleted; set the supplier to inactive instead so history is preserved.

## Annual review
All suppliers with more than ten orders per year are reviewed annually. Suppliers rated D for two consecutive reviews are removed from the approved supplier list.
