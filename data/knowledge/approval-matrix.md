# Requisition Approval Matrix

## Approval limits
Requisitions are approved according to their total estimated amount. Requisitions up to 10,000 are approved by the department approver. Requisitions between 10,001 and 100,000 require approval from the procurement manager. Requisitions above 100,000 require approval from the procurement manager and an administrator.

## Rejected requisitions
A rejected requisition cannot be approved later. The requester must create a new requisition that addresses the rejection reason. Every rejection must include a written reason of at least three characters.

## Segregation of duties
A requester must never approve their own requisition. Users with the viewer role have read-only access and can never create or approve requisitions. Only approvers, procurement managers and administrators can approve or reject requisitions.

## Turnaround
Approvers should act on a submitted requisition within two working days. Requisitions waiting longer than five working days are escalated to the procurement manager.
