# Procurement Policy

## Purpose and scope
This policy governs how every department purchases goods and services. All purchases must be raised as a purchase requisition in the procurement platform before any commitment is made to a supplier. Verbal orders and purchases without a requisition are not permitted.

## Competitive quotations
Purchases with an estimated value above 50,000 must be competitively sourced through a request for quotation (RFQ). At least three active suppliers must be invited to quote. The award decision is based on a combination of total price, delivery lead time and the supplier scorecard rating. A written justification is required when the lowest priced quotation is not selected.

## Emergency purchases
Emergency purchases that prevent plant shutdown or a safety incident may skip the RFQ step. The requester must document the reason, and the procurement manager must approve the purchase within one working day of the emergency order.

## Split orders
Splitting a purchase into several smaller orders to stay below an approval or quotation threshold is prohibited and is treated as a policy violation.
