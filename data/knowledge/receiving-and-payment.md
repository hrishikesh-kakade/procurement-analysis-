# Receiving Goods and Payment

## Goods receipt
Goods must be inspected on arrival and recorded as a delivery against the purchase order. A delivery cannot exceed the quantity that was ordered. When only part of an order arrives, the purchase order becomes partially received and the remaining quantity stays open.

## Discrepancies
Damaged or incorrect goods must be reported to the supplier within three working days of receipt. Damaged goods are not recorded as received quantity.

## Three-way match
Finance pays a supplier invoice only after a three-way match between the purchase order, the goods receipt and the supplier invoice. Invoices without a matching purchase order are returned to the supplier.

## Payment terms
The standard payment term is 30 days from the invoice date. Early payment discounts offered by suppliers are accepted when the discount exceeds the cost of capital, subject to finance approval.

## Cancelling orders
A draft purchase order can be cancelled at any time. An issued purchase order can be cancelled only before any goods have been received. Received purchase orders cannot be cancelled.
